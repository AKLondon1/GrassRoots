# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

The primary user is a busy parent or guardian checking a child’s next football commitment on a phone, often while juggling work, school, transport, and family schedules. The secondary users are volunteer managers, coaches, treasurers, welfare officers, fixture secretaries, and facilities administrators who need dependable club operations without spreadsheets or fragmented message threads. Platform operators support multiple clubs without casual access to sensitive records.

## Product Purpose

GrassRoots is the shared operational home for junior football clubs. It connects events, availability, households, squads, pitches, communications, payments, consent, coaching, and compliance around one source of truth. Success means a parent understands the next event and required action within five seconds, while a volunteer can organise the week from a phone.

## Positioning

GrassRoots turns the complexity of running a community football club into one calm, role-aware next step.

## Brand Personality

Friendly, calm, and assured. The voice uses plain British English, treats children with dignity, respects volunteer time, and communicates safety-sensitive matters without alarmism or euphemism.

## Anti-references

Avoid dark or macho sports styling, public child rankings, council-administration density, football clichés on every surface, generic AI dashboard grids, ornamental glass panels, and interfaces that expose club-wide complexity to parents.

## Design Principles

1. Put the next action before the navigation system.
2. Reveal complexity only to the role that can act on it.
3. Connect every operational workflow to the canonical event record.
4. Make safety, privacy, and permission boundaries visible and dependable.
5. Prefer familiar controls and plain language over clever interface inventions.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Support keyboard and screen-reader use, visible focus, large touch targets, reduced motion, non-colour status cues, accessible alternatives to drag-and-drop, responsive text, clear errors, and logical headings. Children do not require accounts, and adult-to-child private messaging is prohibited.
