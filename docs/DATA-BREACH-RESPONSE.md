# Data-breach response

1. Protect people first; use emergency/safeguarding routes where appropriate.
2. Record time, reporter and an opaque incident reference. Do not copy sensitive bodies into general chat or tickets.
3. Contain: revoke sessions/tokens, suspend affected membership/organisation, rotate secrets, stop jobs/providers and preserve evidence.
4. Assess categories, children/sensitive data, subjects, volume, encryption, access, likely harm and cross-tenant impact with the DPO and safeguarding lead.
5. Restore safe service from verified state and monitor recurrence.
6. The controller/legal adviser decides regulator and data-subject notifications within applicable UK GDPR deadlines; record reasoning even when notification is not required.
7. Notify insurers, processors, FA/County FA or law enforcement where contractually or legally required.
8. Complete lessons learned, control changes and a restore/response exercise.

Maintain out-of-band contact details. The application is not an emergency reporting channel and this playbook requires legal and safeguarding approval.
