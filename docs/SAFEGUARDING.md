# Safeguarding design

Safeguarding concerns and actions are highly restricted records. Ordinary coaches and platform support cannot read concern bodies. Welfare access uses dedicated functions, records metadata-only access outcomes and keeps sensitive text out of normal analytics, notifications and audit metadata. Adult-to-child private messaging does not exist.

Medical information is separate from general player profiles. Emergency views return the minimum operational summary for an authorised event context. Restricted family circumstances are not exposed through household lists or routine messages.

Technology does not decide safeguarding action. Before live use, the club’s designated safeguarding lead must approve roles, escalation routes, emergency contacts, retention, wording and incident handling against current FA, County FA and statutory guidance. Seek immediate professional/emergency help when required; do not rely on this application as an emergency service.
