# UK GDPR readiness checklist

This is an engineering readiness checklist, not legal advice.

- [ ] Controller/processor roles and lawful bases documented per purpose.
- [ ] Privacy notices, child transparency and age-appropriate language approved.
- [ ] DPIA completed for child, medical, safeguarding, AI and support-access processing.
- [ ] Processor agreements and international-transfer safeguards signed.
- [ ] Data minimisation, field access and retention schedules approved.
- [ ] Subject export, correction, deletion, objection and consent withdrawal procedures tested.
- [ ] Legal holds prevent unlawful deletion; anonymisation preserves required finance/safeguarding evidence without retaining unnecessary identity.
- [ ] Private exports expire and are deleted from storage.
- [ ] Breach response and regulator/data-subject notification decisions rehearsed.
- [ ] Production analytics and error tools configured without sensitive bodies.

Account and organisation deletion use a cooling-off period, ownership-transfer guard, retention jobs and inventory-based erasure across export, quarantine and released-private-file buckets. Account holds require a welfare-authorised reviewer, reason and expiry. A human must review conflicts between erasure requests and safeguarding, tax, insurance or legal retention duties.
